Georgia · Workforce · GA-CHILD-001

Georgia Employer Childcare Expense Credit

September 28, 2026 web addition.

Credit amount / calculation

$1,000 per child in the first year and $500 per child in later years for eligible child-care payments; statewide annual cap applies.

Business fit and eligible activity

Forest-products employers making eligible child-care payments for employees can screen this as a workforce-support credit beginning with 2026 tax years.

Eligibility and practical use

This is a general employer workforce credit, not a forestry-specific incentive. It may matter where child care is a real hiring or retention barrier and the employer pays a qualifying child-care facility directly for employees' children.

Illustrative business benefit

A mill makes eligible payments for 12 employees' children in its first qualifying year. At $1,000 per child, the nominal credit would be $12,000 before preapproval, statewide cap, income-tax-liability, and documentation limits.

Timing / first action

Seek preapproval through the Georgia Tax Center before relying on the credit; document eligible payments, covered employees, child age, and the qualifying child-care facility.

Official sources

  • Georgia Department of Revenue — Georgia Employer Childcare Expense Credit (dor.georgia.gov)
