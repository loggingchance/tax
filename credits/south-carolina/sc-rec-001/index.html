South Carolina · Recycling / Large Project · SC-REC-001

Recycling Property Tax Credit

Publication revised September 24, 2026. Sourcebook page 244.

Credit amount / calculation

30% of qualifying investment; can offset multiple state taxes and carry until used.

Business fit and eligible activity

Only very large qualifying recycling facilities will meet the $150 million investment threshold. Qualified investment or approved project activity; confirm eligible property, location and taxpayer type.
