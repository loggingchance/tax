Vermont | research and development | VT-RD-001

Research and Development Tax Credit

Publication revised September 23, 2026. Sourcebook page 264.

View this program in the full 2026 Sourcebook

Credit amount / calculation

For 2026: 27% of the federal research credit attributable to Vermont. Act 164 increases this to 75% for tax years beginning January 1, 2027 or later.

Business fit and eligible activity

Vermont companies receiving the federal research credit can claim a state credit. Qualified research activity. Ordinary production, routine quality control and an equipment purchase alone do not qualify.

Illustrative business benefit

A $20,000 federal research credit attributable to Vermont gives $5,400 at the 2026 rate of 27%. For a tax year beginning in 2027, the enacted 75% rate would give $15,000 on the same assumed federal credit base.

Timing / first action

Tax-year claim: document the eligible expense, hire or placed-in-service date and meet any separate certification deadline. Use the rate for the beginning date of the tax year; the enacted increase is not effective for 2026 tax years. Page
