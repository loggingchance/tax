North Dakota | ND-AUTO-001

Automation Equipment Tax Credit

15% of qualifying automation purchases or capital-lease value; statewide allocation may reduce the award.

Confirm primary-sector certification and equipment eligibility; preserve evidence of improved job quality or productivity. Apply by January 31 for purchases in the preceding calendar year.
