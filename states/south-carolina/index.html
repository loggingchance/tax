South Carolina

Programs

SCSC-ABAND-001

Abandoned Building Revitalization Credit

Credit based on qualified rehabilitation expenditures

Useful where a mill rehabilitates a qualifying abandoned industrial building; notice must precede expenditures.
Fit 2/3Difficulty 3/3Not refundable
SCSC-CAP-001

Capital Investment Credit

0.5% to 2

Directly relevant to mills placing qualified manufacturing and productive equipment in service.
Fit 3/3Difficulty 1/3Not refundable
SCSC-CHILD-001

Employer Child Care Program Credit

50% of eligible child-care startup costs

Forest-products employers establishing or operating qualified child care programs can claim a state credit.
Fit 2/3Difficulty 2/3Not refundable
SCSC-JOBS-001

New Jobs Credit

Per-job amount varies by county

Certain employers creating and maintaining 10+ jobs can receive county-tiered credits.
Fit 2/3Difficulty 2/3Not refundable
SCSC-REC-001

Recycling Property Tax Credit

30% of qualifying investment

Only very large qualifying recycling facilities will meet the $150 million investment threshold.
Fit 1/3Difficulty 3/3Not refundable
SCSC-RD-001

Research Expenses Credit

5% of South Carolina qualified research expenses

Businesses claiming the federal research credit can claim a South Carolina production, routine quality control and an equipment purchase alone do not qualify.
Fit 2/3Difficulty 1/3Not refundable
SCSC-SBJOBS-001

Small Business Jobs Credit

Per-job amount varies by county and wages

Businesses with 99 or fewer employees can qualify with as few as two new full-time jobs.
Fit 3/3Difficulty 2/3Not refundable
SCSC-SOLAR-001

Solar / Small Hydropower / Geothermal Equipment Credit

25% of qualifying system cost

Potentially relevant to qualifying non-biomass energy systems installed at a mill.
Fit 1/3Difficulty 2/3Not refundable
