Oregon | OR-QJ-001

Qualified Jobs Creation Tax Credit

$1,000 per qualifying net new job, up to 10 jobs / $10,000 per taxpayer per tax year; $12.5 million annual statewide cap.

Check application or certification requirements before incurring the qualifying expense or beginning the qualifying activity. 2026 application window: September 1-October 31, 2026.
