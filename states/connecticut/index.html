Connecticut | CT-RD-001

Research and Development Credit

Qualified small businesses generally calculate a 6% tentative credit on eligible Connecticut research expenses; other companies use a tiered schedule. Annual use is restricted.

Tax-year claim: document the eligible expense, hire or placed-in-service date and meet any separate certification deadline. Apply the proper small-business test, eligible expense definition and annual-use calculation.
