Recycling

6 programs connected to recycling. Search results preserve context and do not imply eligibility.

Programs

MTMT-REC-001

Recycling Tax Credit

25% of the first $250

Potentially relevant to qualifying recycling equipment/property used by a wood-products business.
Fit 1/3Difficulty 2/3Not refundable
NJNJ-REC-001

Recycling Equipment Credit

Credit based on eligible recycling equipment under current

Potentially useful for qualifying recycling machinery at a forest-products plant.
Fit 1/3Difficulty 2/3Not refundable
NCNC-RECY-001

Tax Incentives for Major Recycling Facilities

Credit package under Article 3C for qualifying major

Only very large qualifying recycling facilities will meet the scale requirements.
Fit 1/3Difficulty 3/3Not refundable
SCSC-REC-001

Recycling Property Tax Credit

30% of qualifying investment

Only very large qualifying recycling facilities will meet the $150 million investment threshold.
Fit 1/3Difficulty 3/3Not refundable
UTUT-RMDZ-001

Recycling Market Development Zone Tax Credit

Machinery credit

Potentially useful to a wood-residual business operating in a designated zone and making products from qualifying recycled/post-consumer material.
Fit 1/3Difficulty 2/3Not refundable
VAVA-REC-001

Recyclable Materials Processing Equipment Tax Credit

20% of qualifying machinery/equipment purchase price

Potentially relevant to manufacturers producing products from recyclable material; current authorization runs through tax years beginning before 2027.
Fit 1/3Difficulty 3/3Not refundable
